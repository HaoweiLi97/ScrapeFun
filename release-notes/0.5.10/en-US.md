# ScrapeFun 0.5.10

Media libraries scan and load more smoothly, and playback and reading are easier to resume.

## Improvements

- Local library scans spend less time revisiting unchanged folders. A full scan is still available.
- Comic covers, long episode lists, and background refreshes load more smoothly on detail pages.

## Fixes

- Corrected resume positions for some episodes and prompt behavior when moving to the next episode.
- Improved back navigation and transitions into and out of full-screen reading.

## Before updating

- The service restarts during the update, briefly interrupting playback and background tasks. Back up your database and settings first.
